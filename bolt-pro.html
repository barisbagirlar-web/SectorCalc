Engineering Method & Scope

SC-035 calculates thread stress geometry, torsion-corrected permissible assembly preload, minimum/maximum preload from tightening scatter and tightening torque from thread pitch, thread friction and under-head friction diameter. The implied nut factor is reported as a diagnostic, not as a hidden constant.

This module sizes the assembly tightening state. External axial load sharing, clamp reserve, joint separation and fatigue are verified separately by SC-036. Fine threads, prevailing-torque hardware and friction coatings require exact supplier data.
