Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026.

Calculations

Five reference calculators (surface finish, ISO fits, sheet-metal bend, punching force, weld thickness) run without a credit session. Other live calculators require a credit-backed session unlock before results run. After unlock (or on free tools), core engineering math runs in your browser; those calculation inputs are not uploaded to SectorCalc servers for the core math path. Account, wallet, and billing events are processed on our servers (and by Paddle for payments).

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic and product usage.

Payments

Paid credit packs are processed by Paddle as Merchant of Record. Payment data is handled by Paddle under their privacy terms.
