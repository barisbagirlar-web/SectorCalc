Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026.

Calculations

Live calculators require a credit-backed session unlock before results run. After unlock, core engineering math runs in your browser; those calculation inputs are not uploaded to SectorCalc servers for the core math path. Account, wallet, and billing events are processed on our servers (and by Paddle for payments).

Paid credit packs are processed by Paddle as Merchant of Record. Payment data is handled by Paddle under their privacy terms.
