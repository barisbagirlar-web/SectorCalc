What is Process Capability Cpk?

Process Capability Cpk is a statistical measure of how well a stable process fits inside specification limits, calculated as min of (USL-mu)/3sigma and (mu-LSL)/3sigma. In engineering practice, it is used to quantify whether a manufacturing process can consistently produce parts within tolerance bands.

PropertyValue
StandardAIAG, ISO 22514
FormulaCpk = min[(USL-mu)/3s, (mu-LSL)/3s]
UnitsUnitless
Used InQuality control, PPAP, SPC monitoring

How Process Capability Cpk Works

Cpk combines mean location and process spread. A higher Cpk means more of the distribution sits inside the specification.

Stack-up tools often report predicted Cpk or PPM from Monte Carlo results so manufacturing can judge whether the design is producible.

Key Variables

  • USL / LSL: Upper and lower specification limits.
  • σ: Estimated process standard deviation.
  • Cpk: min((USL−μ)/(3σ), (μ−LSL)/(3σ)).

Common Mistakes

  • Computing Cpk from non-stable process data — the index becomes meaningless during special-cause variation.
  • Treating Cpk alone as proof of customer quality without distribution shape checks.
