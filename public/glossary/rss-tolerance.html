What is RSS Tolerance?

RSS Tolerance is Root-Sum-Square statistical tolerancing that assumes independent normal distributions where each tolerance represents approximately plus-minus 3 sigma. In engineering practice, it is used when parts are independently manufactured by capable, centered processes and the quality system accepts statistical tolerancing per ASME Y14.5.

PropertyValue
StandardASME Y14.5
FormulaT_RSS = sqrt(sum(t_i^2))
Unitsmm, um, inch

Why it matters

RSS is the classical statistical stack shortcut under independence assumptions.

Typical mistake

Using RSS when contributors are strongly correlated.

How RSS Tolerance Works

RSS is appropriate when processes are centered and capable, and contributors can be treated as statistically independent. It produces a tighter predicted stack than worst-case.

The method does not replace capability studies. If a process is not near-normal or is strongly biased, RSS can understate risk.

Key Variables

  • t_i: Tolerance half-width of contributor i.
  • T_RSS: Predicted stack tolerance equal to sqrt(sum(t_i^2)).
  • Independence: Assumption that contributors do not share correlated error sources.

Common Mistakes

  • Applying RSS when fixtures, temperature, or shared datums correlate multiple features — predicted yield becomes optimistic.
  • Confusing RSS with a guarantee — RSS is a statistical estimate, not a hard limit like worst-case.
