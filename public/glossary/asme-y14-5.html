What is ASME Y14.5?

ASME Y14.5 is the American standard for dimensioning and tolerancing that defines GD&T symbols, datum reference frames, and tolerance zones. In engineering practice, it is used to communicate design intent unambiguously on engineering drawings across manufacturing and inspection.
