What is Labor Burden Rate?

Labor Burden Rate is the ratio of total labor cost (salary + benefits + taxes + overhead) to base salary, expressing the true cost multiplier. In engineering practice, it is used to calculate loaded labor cost for quoting and project profitability analysis.

PropertyValue
StandardNone (cost accounting)
FormulaBurden Rate = Total Labor Cost / Base Salary
UnitsMultiplier (e.g., 1.8x)
Used InEstimating, project costing, service pricing

Why it matters

Net wage is not employer cost — burden decides quote honesty.

Formula relationship

True hourly = fully loaded monthly cost ÷ paid hours.

Typical mistake

Putting net pay into machine-hour labor share.

How Labor Burden Rate Works

True labor cost is never the paycheck alone. Burden converts gross pay into employer cost per productive hour.

Shop quotes that omit burden silently destroy margin.

Key Variables

  • Gross wage: Base pay before employer on-costs.
  • Burden %: On-cost as a fraction of gross.
  • Billable hours: Hours actually chargeable after indirect time.

Common Mistakes

  • Applying a generic burden percentage that ignores local statutory rates.
  • Dividing annual cost by paid hours instead of productive hours — rate looks artificially low.
