What is VDI 2230 Bolted Joint?

VDI 2230 Bolted Joint is the German guideline for systematic calculation of high-strength bolted joints considering preload, clamping force, and fatigue. In engineering practice, it is used to design bolted connections in mechanical engineering with verified safety margins.

Why it matters

Load factor Φ shows how external load splits between bolt and clamped parts.

Formula relationship

Φ = Ck/(Ck+Cp); Fmax = Fpre + Φ·Fext.

Typical mistake

Ignoring joint opening when Fmin ≤ 0.

How VDI 2230 Bolted Joint Works

It covers preload, embedding losses, stiffness of bolt and clamped parts, and fatigue safety.

Engineers use it to verify that assembly torque produces enough residual clamp load after embedding and external load.

Key Variables

  • F_M: Assembly preload.
  • δ: Elastic resilience of bolt and clamped parts.
  • Safety factors: Against yielding, fatigue, and clamp loss.

Common Mistakes

  • Ignoring embedding (Setzbetrag) — residual preload after assembly is overstated.
  • Using dry friction coefficients for lubricated threads — torque produces the wrong preload.
