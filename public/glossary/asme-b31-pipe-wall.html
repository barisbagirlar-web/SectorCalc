What is ASME B31 Pipe Wall?

ASME B31 Pipe Wall is the ASME B31.3 process piping code requirements for minimum pipe wall thickness considering internal pressure, corrosion, and mill tolerance. In engineering practice, it is used to size process piping and calculate MAWP for chemical and petrochemical plants.

PropertyValue
StandardASME B31.3
Formulat = (P*D)/(2*(S*E*W + P*Y)) + c
Unitsmm, inch
Used InProcess piping, chemical plants, oil & gas

Why it matters

Pressure design thickness is the floor before schedule selection and allowances.

Formula relationship

t = P·D / (2·(S·E·W + P·Y)).

Typical mistake

Forgetting corrosion allowance or mixing OD/ID bases.

How ASME B31 Pipe Wall Works

Wall thickness must resist internal pressure while leaving allowance for corrosion, mechanical strength, and manufacturing undertolerance.

MAWP checks reverse the thickness formula to confirm a selected schedule is adequate.

Key Variables

  • t: Pressure design thickness.
  • S: Allowable stress at design temperature.
  • c: Corrosion / mechanical allowance.

Common Mistakes

  • Selecting pipe schedule from pressure alone without temperature-dependent allowable stress.
  • Forgetting mill tolerance when converting minimum wall to nominal ordered wall.
