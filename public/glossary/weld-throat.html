What is Weld Throat?

Weld Throat is the minimum distance from the root of a fillet weld to its face, typically assumed as 0.707 times leg length for 45 degree equal-leg fillets. In engineering practice, it is used to calculate weld strength and size joints per AWS D1.1 and ISO standards.

PropertyValue
StandardAWS D1.1, ISO 5817
Formulat = 0.707 * a (for 45 deg leg)
Unitsmm, inch
Used InStructural welding, pressure vessels, fabrication

Why it matters

Throat size governs fillet weld strength screening.

Formula relationship

Effective throat relates to leg size and process factors.

Typical mistake

Confusing leg length with throat on unequal-leg fillets.

How Weld Throat Works

For an equal-leg fillet, throat is commonly taken as 0.707 times leg size under idealized geometry.

Codes such as AWS D1.1 and ISO fillet weld rules define minimum throat, allowable stress, and inspection criteria.

Key Variables

  • a (throat): Effective throat thickness.
  • z (leg): Fillet leg length along each member.
  • Utilization: Applied stress divided by allowable stress.

Common Mistakes

  • Sizing from leg length alone without converting to throat for stress checks.
  • Ignoring convexity/concavity and root gaps that reduce effective throat in production welds.
