What is Weld Throat?

Weld Throat is the minimum distance from the root of a fillet weld to its face, typically assumed as 0.707 times leg length for 45 degree equal-leg fillets. In engineering practice, it is used to calculate weld strength and size joints per AWS D1.1 and ISO standards.
