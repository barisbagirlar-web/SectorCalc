What is CNC Feeds and Speeds?

CNC Feeds and Speeds is the cutting parameters (spindle speed and feed rate) that determine material removal rate, surface finish, and tool life in machining operations. In engineering practice, it is used to optimize machining productivity while maintaining tool life and part quality.
