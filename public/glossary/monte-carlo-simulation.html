What is Monte Carlo Simulation?

Monte Carlo Simulation is a computational method that draws random samples per contributor according to assigned distributions and builds an empirical histogram of outcomes. In engineering practice, it is used when distributions are non-normal, truncated, or mixed across components where RSS closed-form math loses accuracy.
