What is Sling Angle Factor?

Sling Angle Factor is the reduction in rated capacity of a sling as the angle from vertical increases, calculated as the cosine of the angle. In engineering practice, it is used to verify safe working loads for multi-leg slings and lifting operations.

PropertyValue
StandardASME B30.9, EN 1492
FormulaSWL_actual = SWL_vertical * cos(theta)
UnitskN, lbf, tonne
Used InLifting, rigging, construction, offshore
