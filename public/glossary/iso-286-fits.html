What is ISO 286 Fits?

ISO 286 Fits is the ISO tolerance system for linear sizes defining fundamental deviations (e.g., H7, g6) and tolerance grades IT01 through IT18. In engineering practice, it is used to specify fit classes between mating parts such as clearance, transition, and interference fits.

PropertyValue
StandardISO 286-1
FormulaUpper/Lower deviation from tables
Unitsmm, um
Used InMechanical design, bearing housings, shaft-bore assemblies
