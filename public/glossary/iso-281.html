What is ISO 281?

ISO 281 is the international standard for rolling bearing rating life that includes life adjustment factors aISO for lubrication and contamination. In engineering practice, it is used to calculate adjusted bearing life Lnm considering operating conditions beyond basic dynamic load rating.

PropertyValue
StandardISO 281:2007
FormulaL_nm = a1 * aISO * L10
Unitsmillions of revolutions, hours
Used InBearing selection, maintenance optimization, reliability

Why it matters

ISO 281 defines rating life used in bearing selection.

Formula relationship

Basic L10 from (C/P)^p; modified life applies aISO factors.

Typical mistake

Setting aISO = 1 in contaminated lubrication.

How ISO 281 Works

ISO 281 defines basic rating life and the framework for modified rating life with lubrication and contamination factors.

Engineers use it to size bearings for required reliability at a stated load spectrum and speed.

Key Variables

  • L10: Basic rating life (90% reliability).
  • Lnm: Modified rating life with aISO adjustments.
  • κ (kappa): Viscosity ratio of actual to required lubricant viscosity.

Common Mistakes

  • Applying ISO 281 static formulas to heavily oscillating or heavily contaminated service without checking limits.
  • Mixing units between kN catalog ratings and N application loads.
