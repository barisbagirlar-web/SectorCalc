What is ASME VIII Pressure Vessel?

ASME VIII Pressure Vessel is the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code Section VIII rules for design, fabrication, inspection, and testing of pressure vessels. In engineering practice, it is used to calculate minimum required thickness for internal pressure shells and verify vessel safety.

PropertyValue
StandardASME Section VIII Division 1
Formulat = (P*R)/(S*E - 0.6*P)
Unitsmm, inch
Used InPressure vessel design, chemical processing, oil & gas
