What is Bearing L10 Life?

Bearing L10 Life is the basic rating life in millions of revolutions that 90 percent of a bearing population will reach or exceed under standard conditions. In engineering practice, it is used to select bearings and validate replacement intervals for rotating equipment.

PropertyValue
StandardISO 281, DIN 622
FormulaL10 = (C/P)^p
Unitsmillions of revolutions, hours
Used InRotating equipment, maintenance planning, reliability engineering
