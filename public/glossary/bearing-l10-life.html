What is Bearing L10 Life?

Bearing L10 Life is the basic rating life in millions of revolutions that 90 percent of a bearing population will reach or exceed under standard conditions. In engineering practice, it is used to select bearings and validate replacement intervals for rotating equipment.

PropertyValue
StandardISO 281, DIN 622
FormulaL10 = (C/P)^p
Unitsmillions of revolutions, hours
Used InRotating equipment, maintenance planning, reliability engineering

Why it matters

L10 is a reliability statistic used to screen catalog capacity versus load and speed.

Formula relationship

L10 = (C/P)^p · 10⁶ revolutions; L10h = L10/(60·n).

Typical mistake

Treating L10 hours as a calendar warranty clock.

How Bearing L10 Life Works

L10 is computed from dynamic load rating C and equivalent dynamic load P using L10 = (C/P)^p with exponent p depending on bearing type.

Modified rating life adjusts L10 with aISO factors for lubrication quality, contamination, and fatigue load limit.

Key Variables

  • C: Basic dynamic load rating.
  • P: Equivalent dynamic bearing load.
  • aISO: Life modification factor from operating conditions.

Common Mistakes

  • Using catalog C without converting loads into the equivalent dynamic load P for the actual duty cycle.
  • Ignoring contamination and viscosity ratio — L10m can be far below basic L10.
