What is Machine Hour Rate?

Machine Hour Rate is the true hourly cost of operating a machine including depreciation, maintenance, power, consumables, and allocated overhead. In engineering practice, it is used for accurate job costing, quoting, and make-vs-buy decisions.

PropertyValue
StandardNone (cost accounting)
FormulaMHR = (Depreciation + Maintenance + Power + Overhead) / Available Hours
UnitsUSD/hour, EUR/hour
Used InJob shops, estimating, cost accounting

Why it matters

Shop rates that ignore ownership or utilization underprice machine time.

Formula relationship

Total €/h = (fixed/hours + energy + labor share) × (1 + overhead%).

Typical mistake

Using planned hours instead of metered annual hours.

How Machine Hour Rate Works

Accurate machine rates prevent underquoting CNC and fabrication work when only direct labor is considered.

Utilization assumptions dominate the rate — idle capital still costs money.

Key Variables

  • Depreciation: Capital recovery per hour of planned use.
  • Utilization: Productive hours / available hours.
  • Overhead: Allocated facility and support costs.

Common Mistakes

  • Using purchase price without residual value and real utilization — rate is fiction.
  • Leaving out power, tooling amortization, or floor space when comparing cells.
