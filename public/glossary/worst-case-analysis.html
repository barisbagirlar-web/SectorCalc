What is Worst-Case Analysis?

Worst-Case Analysis is an accumulation philosophy that assumes every contributor simultaneously sits at its least favorable extreme within the stated tolerance. In engineering practice, it is required for safety-critical mechanisms, small lot sizes, or when customer contract explicitly mandates worst-case accumulation.
