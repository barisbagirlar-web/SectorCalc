About SectorCalc

SectorCalc is a client-side engineering calculator platform for shop-floor and design-review decisions. Every tool uses deterministic decimal arithmetic, visible formulas, and exportable audit trails.

What we build

Tolerance stack-up, CNC feeds and speeds, bearing life, weld sizing, labor costing, and related industrial decision tools — without uploading calculation data to a server.

Academic oversight

Methodology review is supported by Prof. Dr. Neela Nataraj (IIT Bombay) for numerical and statistical rigor where applicable.
