About SectorCalc

SectorCalc builds deterministic industrial engineering calculators with visible formulas, ISO-aligned audit trails, and client-side precision.

What we build

Tolerance stack-up, CNC feeds and speeds, bearing life, weld sizing, labor costing, and related industrial decision tools. Core engineering math for unlocked tools runs in the browser; calculation inputs for that path are not uploaded to SectorCalc servers.

Calculation philosophy

SectorCalc exposes formulas, assumptions, model limits and A1–A5 audit trails so engineers can review deterministic results. Calculator outputs are engineering previews — not a substitute for measured data, manufacturer ratings, governing-code compliance or licensed engineering sign-off.
