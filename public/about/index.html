About SectorCalc

SectorCalc is a client-side engineering calculator platform for shop-floor and design-review decisions. Every tool uses deterministic decimal arithmetic, visible formulas, and exportable audit trails.

What we build

Tolerance stack-up, CNC feeds and speeds, bearing life, weld sizing, labor costing, and related industrial decision tools — without uploading calculation data to a server.

Calculation philosophy

SectorCalc exposes formulas, assumptions, model limits and A1–A5 audit trails so engineers can review deterministic results. Calculator outputs are engineering previews — not a substitute for measured data, manufacturer ratings, governing-code compliance or licensed engineering sign-off.

Academic sources and methodology references

Where tools implement published numerical or statistical methods, SectorCalc documents the equation scope and limitations in-tool. SectorCalc does not claim university certification, PE stamp or third-party engineering approval of shop-floor results.
