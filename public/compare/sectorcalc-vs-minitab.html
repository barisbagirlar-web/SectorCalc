1. Formula Transparency

SectorCalc shows every formula used in the calculation, including unit conversions, reference values, and engine version. The A3 section of the audit trail lists applied formulas exactly as executed by the Decimal engine.

2. Audit Trail & Compliance

SectorCalc generates an A1-A5 audit module with every report: A1 engine identity/version, A2 entered and canonical inputs, A3 applied formulas, A4 assumptions and model boundaries, A5 warnings and limit checks. The report includes deterministic input and output hashes.

3. Data Privacy

All SectorCalc calculations run client-side in the browser. No part dimensions, process data, or results are transmitted to any server. This is critical for ITAR-controlled, proprietary, or competitive-sensitive designs.

4. When to Choose Which

Choose SectorCalc if:

  • You need a defensible audit trail for customer review or internal QA.
  • You work with mixed metric/imperial units and need canonical reference values.
  • You cannot send part dimensions or process data to a cloud server.
  • You need deterministic, reproducible Monte Carlo results with seeded iteration.
  • You prefer pay-per-use credits over a monthly subscription.

Choose Minitab if:

  • You need general statistical process control (SPC) beyond engineering calculators.
  • Your organization has enterprise Minitab licenses and dedicated statisticians.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import my Minitab data into SectorCalc?

No direct import exists. However, you can manually enter your Minitab inputs into SectorCalc and compare results. The deterministic engine will show exactly where the calculations diverge and why.

Does SectorCalc replace Minitab entirely?

Not entirely. SectorCalc specializes in deterministic, client-side engineering calculations with full audit trails. Minitab excels at statistical analysis. Many engineers use both: Minitab for design, SectorCalc for audit-ready verification.

Is SectorCalc accepted for PPAP submissions?

SectorCalc outputs are labeled "engineering preview -- predicted from drawing tolerances and chosen distributions, not measured data." They are not a substitute for measured SPC or a licensed engineer sign-off. Use them to prioritize capability studies and justify tolerance changes before PPAP.
