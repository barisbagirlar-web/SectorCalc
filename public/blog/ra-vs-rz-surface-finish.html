Ra vs Rz: which surface finish number do you release?

Direct answer: Ra (arithmetical mean) and Rz (average peak-to-valley) describe different statistics of the same profile. You cannot treat a Ra callout as an Rz limit without an explicit conversion assumption — and that assumption belongs on the traveler, not in tribal memory.

Why shops argue about this

Design often inherits Ra from a CAD material note. Inspection may report Rz because the instrument or customer standard prefers peak-based metrics. When someone “converts by feel,” scrap and supplier fights follow.

What to do on the shop floor

  1. Read the governing surface-texture standard named on the drawing.
  2. Confirm which parameter the acceptance criteria actually use.
  3. If you must translate between parameters, use a converter that shows the assumption — then validate against measured process data.
