Tolerance Stack-Up Audit Template

A practical checklist for APQP / customer reviews of linear stack-up studies.

  1. List every contributor with nominal, tolerance, sign, and distribution.
  2. State which method is governing: worst-case, RSS, or Monte Carlo.
  3. Record seed, iteration count, and engine version for Monte Carlo.
  4. Export predicted gap/interference, Cpk/PPM if used, and warnings.
  5. Attach input/output hashes from the deterministic report.
