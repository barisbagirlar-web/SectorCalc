ISO 286 Quick Reference

Use this page as a pocket reference when selecting ISO 286 fit classes during design reviews.

Hole-basis starting points

  • H7/g6 — common clearance fit for sliding assembly.
  • H7/k6 — transition fit for location with light press.
  • H7/p6 — interference fit for permanent location (check stress).
