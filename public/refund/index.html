Refund & Billing

Last updated: 28 July 2026. Paid credit packs are sold through Paddle as Merchant of Record.

Merchant of Record

Paddle handles checkout, tax, invoices, and payment-method storage. SectorCalc does not receive or store full card numbers. Billing receipts and VAT invoices come from Paddle.

Credits

Purchased calculator credits do not expire. Unused credits remain on the account wallet until consumed by unlock events. Free reference tools do not spend credits.

Refunds

  • Refund requests for unused or defective purchases follow Paddle’s Merchant of Record refund policy.
  • Open a request from your Paddle receipt email, or contact support@sectorcalc.com with the Paddle order ID.
  • If credits were already consumed on calculator unlocks, refunds may be partial or declined per MoR rules and usage.

Chargebacks

Disputed charges are processed by Paddle. Contact support first so we can reconcile wallet state before a chargeback escalates.
