Method & Scope

SC-010 normalizes pay frequency to monthly net pay, grosses that amount up using the selected reference regime, adds employer statutory burden, benefits, severance, overtime and amortized recruitment cost, then enforces conservation between the line-item breakdown and the reported true monthly cost.

Country rates are planning references, not statutory payroll filing rules. Progressive brackets, wage bases, caps and industry-specific funds must be verified against current local law and payroll provider data before contractual use.
