Engineering Method & Scope

SC-001 calculates allowable weld stress from the entered strength and safety factor, derives the required throat from design load and effective weld length, converts throat demand to leg size for an equal-leg fillet, and selects the governing value between load demand and the minimum-leg reference.

Weld-group eccentricity, fatigue category, base-metal rupture, HAZ properties, fit-up, WPS/PQR qualification and NDT acceptance remain separate checks before production release.
