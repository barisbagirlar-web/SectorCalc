Engineering Method & Scope

SC-021 uses ISO 281 basic rating life and modified rating life. The user enters the exact bearing catalog ratings C, C0 and Cu, actual radial/axial loads, speed, operating viscosity, reliability and contamination factor. The engine calculates equivalent dynamic load, L10, reference viscosity, κ, aISO, Lnm and static safety s0.

Geometry alone cannot establish catalog ratings. Misalignment, internal clearance/preload, cage limits, high-speed centrifugal effects and variable load spectra remain outside ISO 281 basic life and require manufacturer software or ISO/TS 16281-level analysis where relevant.
