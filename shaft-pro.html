Engineering Method & Scope

The engine calculates torque from power and speed, a simply-supported midspan bending moment, modified endurance strength using Marin surface/size/reliability factors, then independently solves DE-Goodman fatigue diameter and the ASME allowable-stress shaft equation. The larger requirement is rounded up to a preferred diameter and rechecked for fatigue safety, static yield and beam deflection.

Keyways, shoulders, fillet stress concentrations, non-midspan gears/pulleys, bearing reactions, whirling/critical speed and detailed torsional deflection remain separate design checks before drawing release.
